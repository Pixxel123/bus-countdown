Ways to show your bus

You’re on the 517, 2 minutes from Wexley (Stop D), and the 566 comes 6 minutes after you get there. Five ways the island could say so, weighed with impeccable’s quieter and distill guidance: show hierarchy through shape and position rather than colour, and leave out whatever isn’t needed. The island has C, since 4.30.

    The next bus on your route

    Riding the 517 into Wexley, the island also lists the next 517, 18 minutes behind you. Shown like any other bus, it can look like yours. Three variants made with impeccable’s generate method: the island’s identity written down first, then each variant changes one different thing (hierarchy, colour, structure) and is checked against that identity. The island has 2, since 4.31.

    Identity kept in every variant: a black capsule 30 dp high round the camera; white 14 px bold minutes; a solid TfL-red route badge; the destination in 12 px #B7BFCD; the bus after in 11 px #9AA3B5; a 1.5 px white ring for the stop letter; 4 px dots, #5A6376 with the active one white; no shadows or decoration; terse numbers.
