    Where should the pill sit, and what should it say?

    The first design mockup, before the island was built. Top of a Pixel 8 Pro at true scale, 448 dp wide. The pill rotates through the routes at a stop (or your trains at a station), falling back to the timetable when live times are missing. Short swipe to change route, long swipe to dismiss, hold for the opposite stop. Switch to Trains to explore ideas for showing status and delays. Sample data.
